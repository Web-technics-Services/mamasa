Khmer Food with a Rooftop View

At Mama Sa, dinner brings Cambodian flavors together with open-air views and the slower rhythm of an evening in Kampot.

Published by Mama Sa Hotel & Restaurant · September 30, 2026 · 3 minute read

Khmer cooking is at the center of the Mama Sa rooftop experience. The restaurant offers a welcoming place to discover Cambodian dishes while looking out across the Kampot landscape.

A table of Cambodian flavors

Khmer cuisine balances fresh herbs, aromatics, savory elements, gentle sweetness, acidity, and spice. The result is food with layers rather than a single dominant flavor. Sharing several dishes is a good way to experience those contrasts together.

The restaurant menu presents local dishes in Khmer and English. Because ingredients and availability can change, the current menu page is the best source before ordering.

Why the rooftop setting matters

The rooftop changes dinner from a simple stop into time set aside. Panoramic mountain and countryside views create a calm backdrop, particularly as the day cools. Guests can arrive, settle in, and let the meal unfold without rushing.

Hotel guests have the convenience of dining where they stay. Visitors from elsewhere in Kampot are welcome to contact Mama Sa about a table.

Planning your meal

  • Review the menu and restaurant gallery online.
  • Contact Mama Sa to confirm current hours and table availability.
  • Mention dietary needs when reserving and again before ordering.
  • Allow time to enjoy the view as well as the meal.
Reservations and questions: Call +855 86 582 829 or email info@mamasa.site.

Pair dinner with a stay

Mama Sa combines the rooftop restaurant with comfortable rooms below. If you are planning a Kampot visit, booking both in one place makes for an easy evening: dinner upstairs, then a quiet room without another journey.
