What to Expect from a Stay at Mama Sa

A straightforward introduction to our Kampot retreat, from the feel of the rooms to dining upstairs and making a direct reservation.

Published by Mama Sa Hotel & Restaurant · September 30, 2026 · 4 minute read

Mama Sa is a hotel and retreat in Kampot, Cambodia, created around simple comfort and warm hospitality. It brings guest rooms and a rooftop Khmer restaurant together in one relaxed setting.

Comfortable, light-filled rooms

Our rooms use warm wood tones, soft linens, and practical amenities to create a comfortable place to return to after exploring Kampot. Current room choices include one-bed and two-bed configurations, with fan or air-conditioning options shown on the booking page.

Room types, prices, and availability can change. The direct booking form lets you choose your preferred dates and room, after which the Mama Sa team confirms availability with you.

A calm atmosphere

Mama Sa is shaped by an appreciation for Cambodian hospitality and Khmer design. The atmosphere is intended to feel unhurried: a place to rest between outings, enjoy the surrounding greenery, and share a meal without needing to travel again at the end of the day.

Rooftop Khmer dining

The rooftop restaurant serves Khmer and Cambodian dishes in a panoramic setting. It is part of the Mama Sa experience, but it also welcomes people looking for a meal in Kampot. Visit the dining page for the current menu and restaurant views.

Planning dinner? Contact Mama Sa on +855 86 582 829 to confirm current restaurant information or a reservation.

Finding Mama Sa

The hotel is in Kampot at coordinates 10.5746545, 104.1722945. The homepage includes an embedded map and a direct link to Google Maps, which is the clearest way to review the route from your current location.

How direct booking works

  • Open the Mama Sa booking page.
  • Enter your contact details and preferred check-in and check-out dates.
  • Select a room option and provide any useful notes.
  • Send the request; the Mama Sa team will contact you to confirm availability.

You can also email info@mamasa.site or contact the hotel by phone, Telegram, or WhatsApp using the details on the booking page.
