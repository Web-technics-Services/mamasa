The Kampot Culinary Experience

Set high above Fish Island (Traeuy Kaoh), our open-air restaurant is widely known as having a great sunset view in Kampot. Dining here is designed to be an immersive experience that pairs visual beauty with Cambodia’s rich culinary history.

Our kitchen celebrates authentic Khmer cuisine using market-fresh Kampot ingredients, including the world-famous Kampot pepper. Whether you are savoring our traditional Fish Amok curry or enjoying a premium steak, every dish is crafted with quality ingredients and profound respect for local recipes.

As evening falls, our daily musical mixes provide the perfect atmospheric soundtrack to accompany your meal while you take in sweeping panoramic mountain and Fish Island views.
