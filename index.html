Our Story

Where Tradition Embraces Serenity

Mama Sa is more than a retreat, it is a celebration of Cambodia's soul. Nestled within lush tropical gardens on the serene Fish Island, our property honors the architectural grace of traditional Khmer design, where every timber beam and hand-carved detail tells a story of heritage and craftsmanship.

Here, time moves gently. The scent of frangipani floats through open pavilions, while the rhythm of rural Cambodian life creates a symphony of peace. Explore our on-site mini-market, book local tours at our excursion desk, or simply relax with our free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property. We invite you to experience the warmth of Mama Sa's embrace.

Accommodations

Sanctuaries of Comfort

Each of our 10 residences at Mama Sa is thoughtfully designed to offer modern luxury within an authentic cultural setting.

Cozy, Light-Filled Rooms

Private sanctuaries featuring warm wood tones, soft linens, air conditioning, and hot water. Every room includes a private bathroom, free toiletries, a desk, and a kettle with complimentary tea and coffee. To ensure a truly peaceful and fully unplugged escape, our rooms purposefully exclude TVs and refrigerators. Free private parking is available for all guests.

Culinary Heritage

A Taste of Cambodia

Our stunning rooftop restaurant and lounge, known locally as "Kampot's finest," offers an unforgettable dining experience. Savor authentic Khmer flavors and Western dishes while taking in unparalleled panoramic views. Look to the front and right to see the majestic Bokor Mountain, gaze left toward the peaks of Coconut Mountain (Phnom Doang), and look front-left to catch a sweeping view of Phu Quoc Island, Vietnam. It is the perfect spot to enjoy daily musical mixes and spectacular Kampot sunsets.
